Frequently Asked Questions.

Practical answers to common FedRAMP, CMMC, and compliance questions—focused on how assessments actually work, not marketing myths.

Control inheritance is determined by reviewing the FedRAMP System Security Plan (SSP) and its control implementation summaries. Inherited controls are those fully implemented by the underlying cloud service provider and verified as part of their FedRAMP authorization. Customers are still responsible for configuring and operating their portion of shared controls and fully implementing customer-responsibility controls.
