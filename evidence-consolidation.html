Where 320 assessment objectives actually get their evidence.

Every objective in NIST SP 800-171A has to be evidenced by something. Grouped by what could produce that evidence, 320 objectives collapse into six machine-readable collection classes — and one bucket that no collector reaches. Hover a family or a class to isolate it.

Attributions derived from one reference architecture. In a different estate they must be re-derived — the enforcement point for an objective depends on what is actually deployed. Objectives with more than one designated primary appear as more than one strand.
