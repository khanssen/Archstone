Contact

Questions about NIST SP 800-171, FedRAMP or GovRAMP, or about something in our research? Send a message below or email us directly. Messages go straight to Karen Stanford, Archstone's founder and principal.

What helps in a first message

  • The requirement. The framework or contract clause you're working toward, for example DFARS 252.204-7012, a CMMC Level 2 designation, or a FedRAMP or GovRAMP authorization.
  • The environment. Roughly how many users and systems are in scope, and which cloud services you rely on.
  • The date. Any deadline you're working against: a contract award, an assessment, an annual affirmation.
