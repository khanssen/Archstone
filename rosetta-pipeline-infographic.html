Read the numbers with these limits

Findings are emission claims, not compliance claims — they state what the system reports, not whether any organization conforms.

  • One reference environment (n = 1): Windows/Azure with a FedRAMP-Moderate backend. The method generalizes; the integers may not.
  • 13 dispositions are pending verification against the April 2025 ODP memorandum and are held at "partial" until a published value is confirmed.
  • Corrections are retained visibly in per-objective rationales, including a boundary-assumption error affecting 16 Physical Protection objectives.
  • Definition-shaped objectives count as reportable only where a published referent exists — an unfloored setting ratifies itself and reports nothing.
