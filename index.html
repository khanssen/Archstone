What we do

  • CUI compliance

    Your contract requires NIST SP 800-171. We build the whole program: gap assessment, controls, documentation, and the evidence to back it, most of it pulled from your systems rather than out of your team's week.

    Selling cloud services to federal agencies means an authorization package that survives a 3PAO. We have built them from the assessor's side of the table. On FedRAMP 20x we advise on what the change means for your program, and build against it on request.
